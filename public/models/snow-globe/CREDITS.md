# Christmas Snow Globe: 3D asset credits

The snow globe card (`snow-globe`) includes the third-party assets below. Each one was modified by mawica.: resized to fit the globe, decimated,
re-textured or restyled, and combined with mawica.'s own procedural models (glass, base, trees, snowman, presents and terrain).
